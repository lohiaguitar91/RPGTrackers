The premise

This run treats Meetra Surik the way the post-game Legends material (the Revan novel, the Old Republic comics, Wookieepedia's compiled account) treats her: a Consular first, a swordswoman second. She trained under Kavar and could have been a Guardian, but her real weapon is the Force — Battle Meditation, Force Barrier, Energy Resistance, and eventually Sever Force are the things she's actually known for. The lightsaber is a last-resort tool, not the build.

Her canon second saber (built under Kreia's guidance, during the game) is a double-bladed blue-blade lightsaber — that's the target loadout here, not a green or cyan one. Mechanically that means: take the minimum feat investment to make a double blade function safely, and spend everything else on Wisdom, Force powers, and Charisma-driven social skills (Persuade, Awareness) that reflect her being a wartime leader people followed.

What "comic-accurate, near-light" means here

Legends canon settles Surik as Light Side by the end of the Jedi Civil War era material — she goes on to search for Revan and eventually helps against the True Sith. She's not written as a flawless saint, though: she's blunt, she doesn't fully trust the Council, and she doesn't let Kreia's nihilism go unchallenged even while learning from her. So the target isn't a maxed 100 Light "space Buddha" run — it's a strong, consistent Light alignment (roughly 75–90 on the LS/DS meter) reached through decisive, sometimes hard-edged good choices, not passive niceness.

Rule of thumb: when a dialogue option is "the merciful/fair choice" vs "the naive/doormat choice," take the merciful one — Surik is compassionate, not a pushover. That reads as her, and it keeps LS gains coming from meaningful choices instead of grinding minor toggles.

01Attributes

A caster-Consular spread. Just enough DEX/CON to not get chain-stunned, everything spare into WIS (power DCs, bonus FP as Jedi Master) and CHA (Persuade, and it's true to her leader characterization).

STRDEXCONINTWISCHA

Keep WIS highest and STR lowest, but don't drop STR below 10 — the double blade multiplies your Strength modifier by 1.5 on the main hand, so a negative modifier gets amplified. 10 is neutral; 9 is an active penalty.

The INT question. INT 10 gives no bonus skill points, and Consular is already a 4-points-per-level class. Raising INT to 12 buys roughly +1 skill point per level — around 25 extra ranks across the run. If you want T3's full upgrade chain (see Skills below), take the INT 12 and pull the point from CHA. If you're happy delegating tech work, INT 10 is fine and CHA 15 does more for you.

03Feats — the "surgical" list

The double blade genuinely does use the Two-Weapon Fighting line, not the Dueling line. The engine treats a double-bladed weapon as two weapons: equipping one applies the standard −6 main hand / −10 off hand penalty, and the TWF feats are what reduce it. Double-bladed weapons also count as "balanced," which shaves the penalty further on top of the feats.

  • Lightsaber Proficiency — granted automatically to Jedi classes.
  • Two-Weapon Fighting — take as soon as available. Without it you're swinging at −6/−10.
  • Finesse: Lightsabers — higher priority than Improved TWF lets attack rolls use Dexterity instead of Strength. With DEX 14 and STR 10 that's a straight +2 to hit, and it's the feat that makes a low-Strength saber user viable at all.
  • Improved Two-Weapon Fighting — nice once feat slots are genuinely spare. Third priority.
  • Force Focus / Force Mastery lines — boost power DC; the actual backbone of the build.
  • Skip entirely: Master Two-Weapon Fighting, Dueling line (it's for a single one-handed blade, so it does nothing for a double), Power Attack, ranged feats.
Damage caveat: the double blade's main hand applies 1.5× your Strength modifier, so a dumped Strength is worse here than it would be with a single saber. That's the argument for STR 10 over STR 9 — a negative modifier gets multiplied. Damage still won't be this character's strong suit, and that's fine; Finesse fixes the part that matters, which is landing the hit at all.

05Skills — what's safe to skip, what isn't

The general rule in TSL: most tech work can be delegated. T3-M4 maxes Computer Use, Repair and Security effortlessly thanks to his INT upgrades, and you can swap him in at almost any terminal or locked door. So Computer Use and Security are cheap to under-invest in — with three specific exceptions.

Skills the PC must have, because nobody can cover for you:

SkillTargetWhy
PersuadeAs high as you can carryNever delegable — every dialogue check is yours. Also the peaceful resolution to a lot of encounters, which is how a Light run avoids unnecessary fights. Canon-appropriate: Persuade and Awareness are named as Surik's two best skills.
Awareness15+ (11 early)Gates dialogue, notably with Zez-Kai Ell. 11 is the threshold for Kreia's listening lesson ("I heard her thoughts") and for a Mira influence option that makes her Jedi training much easier. Get to 11 early.
Treat InjuryModestCheap, thematic for a healer, boosts medpac effectiveness.

The three places delegation actually fails:

  • Visquis's lair / Jekk'Jekk Tarr, Nar Shaddaa — the real trap you go in without T3 or HK, so their Security is unavailable. If you want the sliced routes here rather than fighting through, the points have to be on you.
  • Onderon Palace — several worthwhile Security doors, but you can bring a droid, so this one's fine to delegate.
  • T3-M4's full upgrade chain — needs roughly 21 Computer Use and 21 Repair on your own character, since T3 can't upgrade himself. The single most expensive skill commitment in the game.
The honest tradeoff. On a 4-points-per-level Consular with INT 10, you cannot have Persuade, Awareness, and 21/21 Computer Use and Repair. Something gives. Recommendation for this run: take Persuade and Awareness to the cap, skip the 21/21 T3 chain, and skip Security entirely rather than half-funding it. You lose T3's stat upgrades and the moving-meditation reward, a real but contained loss; you keep every dialogue option, which is what a Consular playthrough is built around. If T3's chain matters to you, that's the case for INT 12 at creation.
The cross-class rank cap changes the Security answer. Class skills cap at level + 3; cross-class skills cap at half that. For a Consular, Persuade, Awareness, Treat Injury, Computer Use and Repair are class skills — so your two priorities cost one point per rank and can be raised to the full cap. Security is cross-class: double cost, and hard-capped around 14 ranks at level 25 no matter how much you spend. That's likely short of the hardest slicing checks even before you account for the doubled price. So don't half-invest in Security hoping to cover Nar Shaddaa — either commit early and lean on gear bonuses, or skip it and fight through Visquis's lair. Half-measures buy you nothing here.

Confirm at character creation: the level-up screen shows class skills at 1 point per rank and cross-class at 2. If Security shows as a class skill on your build, ignore the above and buy it freely.

Cross-class ranks are also not retroactive, so anything cross-class has to be bought as you go, never caught up later. Computer Use, Repair and Persuade are the only three skills that affect XP gain.

06Her double blue

Build the double-bladed hilt as soon as parts allow (Telos/Dantooine workbenches), then carry it for the rest of the game. Use a blue color crystal — this matches the second lightsaber established for her post-exile in Revan, built under Kreia's tutelage.

  • Color crystal: blue.
  • Upgrade crystals: favor save-DC / Wisdom-adjacent bonuses or Force-cost reduction over raw melee damage — lean the item bonuses the same direction as the build.
  • Don't sink credits into a second lightsaber or a blaster loadout. One weapon, minimally invested, is the point.

Planned order

After the fixed opening (Peragus → Harbinger → Telos), the middle of the game opens up. This order ramps difficulty sensibly and hits companion beats roughly in the order their stories want to be told.

1Telos — Citadel Station + Restoration Zone

Fixed / opening

Recruits Bao-Dur and unlocks the Ebon Hawk properly. Bring Kreia + Atton — both have a lot of early commentary here. Talk to Kreia after every major choice; her earliest influence gates are here.

2Dantooine

Recommended first open planet

Lower difficulty spike, and thematically right — Surik's old stomping ground, and it gives Kreia room to teach without the party in immediate danger. Bring Kreia + Bao-Dur (his reaction to the ruined Enclave matters), rotating in Mical once recruited.

3Nar Shaddaa

Recruits Mira, Visas Marr, and the G0-T0 thread. Bring Atton (his underworld past pays off here) and Bao-Dur — both have multiple influence spots on this planet specifically. Side with Mira over Hanharr; don't let his recruitment content pressure you into a dark resolution.

4Onderon & Dxun

Bring Mandalore on Dxun (his commentary on the old war is his best material) plus Bao-Dur, who built the mines here during the Mandalorian Wars, and Mira for her influence check. In Iziz, Mandalore is effectively mandatory. This is also where Battle Meditation gets its narrative payoff.

5Korriban

Save for last of the three

The heaviest Dark Side pressure in the game — arrive at higher level with alignment already solidly Light. Note that Kreia can't accompany you into the tomb, though she still speaks through the Force. Bring Visas plus Atton, Mira, or HK-47 for the atmosphere dialogue.

6Endgame — Ravager → Trayus Academy → Malachor V

Bring whichever two companions have the least-resolved arcs going in (commonly Visas and Atton, or Visas and Bao-Dur) so their content triggers before the finale. Kreia is locked in for the true finale regardless.

Jedi training — what this run can and can't get

Companion Jedi training is gated on your influence with that companion individually — not on Kreia's influence bar, and not on the prestige class. Aiming Kreia at "high, not max" costs you none of these. If anything, a committed Light run makes them easier, because most of their positive-influence triggers are the same merciful/decisive choices this build is already making.

Watch-out: if your influence with Visas runs well ahead of another companion's, it can stall their conversation chains. Rotate deliberately rather than defaulting to the same party every planet.

T3-M4 and HK-47 aren't alignment-gated at all — their best content (T3's repair upgrades, HK's "Jedi Killing Lessons") is skill-check and availability based, so this run keeps both in full.

Keeping the party Light

Companion alignment tracks yours through influence: high influence pulls them toward your alignment, low influence lets them drift the other way. Priorities for this run:

  • Bao-Dur & T3-M4 — both can flip Dark through careless dialogue (notably the Czerka scientist and the Peragus fuel line). Always take the kind/patient option with them specifically.
  • Atton & Mical — their arcs are explicitly about choosing a side; consistent Light choices resolve them cleanly by the finale.
  • Visas Marr — her whole arc is Sith-to-Light; bring her to Korriban and Malachor and talk to her after.
  • Mira — responds to competence and mercy, both of which this build already leans into.

Maximizing story payoff, not just influence numbers

  • Bao-Dur → Dantooine and Dxun; both tie directly to his backstory.
  • Mical → anything Jedi-Council or Jedi-history related; his dialogue is built around lore commentary.
  • Atton → Nar Shaddaa and pazaak/underworld content, and keep him in rotation generally since much of his arc is proximity-gated rather than location-gated.
  • Visas → Korriban and Malachor for her strongest unique lines.
  • Mandalore → Dxun and any Mandalorian-Wars conversation triggers.

The actual tradeoff

Worth being straight about this: Kreia's influence system runs largely opposite to a clean Light run. You gain influence with her mostly through Dark-flavored or cynical remarks, and lose it with straightforwardly Light ones — she scolds you for "the charity act" and rewards agreeing with her bleaker read of events. A handful of gains are alignment-neutral (simply talking to her, the listening lessons, a few Light-compatible philosophical agreements), but the bulk of a maxed bar comes from leaning into her worldview at moments that also cost Light Side points or push a companion Dark.

Recommendation: don't chase max. Maxing her would mean repeatedly taking the darker remark in conversations that also gate Bao-Dur's, T3's, and Atton's alignment — working directly against both the near-Light goal and the companion Jedi training above. It's the one system in the game genuinely built to fight a clean Light playthrough.

Why "high" still unlocks nearly everything

Her lore conversations are gated on influence being extreme in either direction — very high or very low both pass, and the middle fails. The thresholds:

ConversationNeeds
"Did you know Atris at all?">60 or <40
How Revan amassed his force>65 or <35
What the Sith who pursue you are>70 or <30
"What are you — Jedi or Sith?">89 or <11
"Did you know Revan?" / "You trained him?">90 or <10

Starting influence is 50, and gains/losses move in steps of 8. Banking just the four free gains available on a Mira run puts you at 82 without a single dark choice — clearing every gate except the top two. Add the scattered no-cost conversational gains and low 90s is reachable while staying firmly Light; you'd only need to concede the charity-lecture line to get there comfortably, and that one is worth refusing on principle.

The one real sacrifice: if you refuse the charity lecture (−8) and refuse the Visas sacrifice (−8), you're spending 16 points and may land in the mid-70s to mid-80s. That costs you the "Did you know Revan / I trained him" reveal at >90. It's excellent lore, but it's also the one thing this run trades away — and Surik refusing to call her friends disposable is more in character than hearing one more Revan story.

Aim high, not max

  • Talk to Kreia constantly — pure conversation and the listening lessons are free influence at no alignment cost.
  • Take the Light-compatible agreement lines when they appear (e.g. "no one is beyond redemption" at the tomb of Ajunta Pall with sufficient Awareness; agreeing with her reasoning rather than her methods).
  • Skip gains that require letting someone die, lying to an NPC for gain, or telling her charity is for suckers — those double as Dark Side and companion-corruption triggers.
  • Land at "high influence, most of her training and dialogue unlocked." That's the version of this relationship where Surik learns from Kreia without becoming her.
